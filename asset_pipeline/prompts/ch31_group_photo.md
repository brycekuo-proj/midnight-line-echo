## Primary request

Create the canonical in-world four-person group photograph revealed by EVA at the end of CH3-1. It must read as an authentic ordinary phone photo that was later archived inside the chat, not promotional art.

## Character and composition lock

- Use the approved K, Lin Yuqing, and EVA anchor images as identity references.
- K appears normal and recognizable.
- Lin Yuqing appears normal and recognizable.
- The player occupies one position as a naturally integrated but completely faceless dark human silhouette; no eyes or features.
- EVA stands at the far right in a simple white long dress, smiling gently.
- EVA should look subtly composited into the existing photo after it was taken: slight edge/lighting mismatch only, never a loud glitch.
- Casual group-photo framing, medium-wide landscape crop, ordinary indoor or sheltered-night setting, all four figures readable on a phone.

## Style lock

Low-saturation realistic evidence photography, restrained contrast, natural phone-camera grain, quiet psychological unease. No anime, illustration, glossy poster lighting, gore, monster, extra people, text, watermark, or new story clues.
